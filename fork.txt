It's actually quite easy! Just follow these steps to get your portfolio live:

1. GitHub Access: Ensure you have a GitHub account and a basic understanding of how to manage repositories.
2. Fork the Repo: Fork the project at https://github.com/AlexOfficial4493/About-Me to your own account.
3. Get the Files: Download the repository files or clone them to your local machine.
4. Configure Your Link: Open config.txt and replace the URL with the link to your own about.txt. (Crucial: Use the "Raw" link from GitHub).
5. Personalize: Edit about.txt with your own info. You can use template.txt as a guide for the formatting.
6. Deploy: Upload config.txt, fork.html, and index.html to your favorite host (Netlify is highly recommended).
7. Support: If something breaks, don't panic. Join the community on Discord for help: https://discord.gg/JdKxQKXCxj
8. Patience: If changes don't show up instantly, just wait a few minutes. raw.githubusercontent.com caching can be slow!

Enjoy your new portfolio! (:
